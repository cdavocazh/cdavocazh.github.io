# About

products. Currently Staff Product Manager (Growth) at DCS Group (DeCard) in
Singapore, owning the end-to-end growth funnel for a B2C PayFi / crypto-card
product — acquisition, KYC, card issuance, spending, retention — plus the
rewards, loyalty, and attribution stack behind it.

Outside work I build multi-agent pipelines for systematic trading analysis,
macro market intelligence, and financial research — combining LLM orchestration
with quantitative data workflows. Earlier: strategy & growth at DeGate
(a DEX with ~$100M TVL at peak), product for OKX's bot-trading ecosystem
($1B daily trading volume), growth product & analytics at Yara International,
and data science before that.
